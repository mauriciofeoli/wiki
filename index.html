About

Hi, I'm Maurício. I work as a QA Engineer at NTT DATA on payments projects, and I previously worked in telecom. Day to day, I automate web and API tests with Cypress, Playwright and Postman, running in Jenkins and Bitbucket pipelines.

Before moving into QA, I built APIs on Apigee. That background helps me understand the integrations I test, not just how the screen behaves. I'm currently studying Python and the use of AI in testing, such as agents that generate test cases and the evaluation of LLMs.
